### Three problems I fix

- **Independent practices:** your software doesn't fit how your practice works.
- **Clinic groups and wellness brands:** patients find you, then slip away.
- **Healthtech startups:** your AI works in the demo, not in the clinic.

### How I build

- **Decision support, not decision making.** The clinician reviews and signs off on everything the software drafts.
- **Rules before models.** Red flags and safety checks are deterministic; AI drafts on top of them.
- **Alongside the EHR, not instead of it.** Clinics keep their records system.
- **Patient data stays behind sign-in,** kept to the minimum and logged.

The thinking behind it: [the Decision Cost Framework](https://ganeshdatta.me/decision-cost-framework/), and [free tools](https://ganeshdatta.me/tools/) to score your own workflows.
